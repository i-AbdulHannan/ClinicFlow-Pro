Security by design

Healthcare-grade security practices, not certifications we don't hold.

Concrete, verifiable controls — not generic claims. Formal compliance depends on the hosting environment and the clinic's own policies.

ControlWhat is implemented
AuthenticationBcrypt password hashing, 10-character minimum policy, generic login errors
Brute force5 failed attempts per 15 minutes per username and per IP → temporary lockout
Two-factor authOptional TOTP for admin and superadmin, enforced at login when enabled
SessionsHttpOnly + SameSite cookies, idle timeout, absolute timeout, revocation on deactivate/delete
CSRFToken required on every POST/PUT/PATCH/DELETE and every state-changing GET
Access controlRole based, clinic-scoped queries, ownership checks on every download

AuditActivity log + security event log (append-only)

Why ClinicFlow Pro exists

Built after seeing how much clinic work still depends on disconnected systems.

The idea came from a practical problem: many clinics still manage important work through paper registers, scattered spreadsheets, phone calls and handwritten prescriptions — while large healthcare systems are built for enterprise environments, not the day to day reality of smaller clinics.

The product direction was simple: build a clinic management system that is practical for local workflows, role aware, communication friendly and broad enough to cover the operational journey from registration to follow up.

That shaped the four dashboards, PKR pricing, WhatsApp reminders, patient timeline, digital prescriptions, lab reports, refill tracking, loyalty and rewards, analytics and AI assisted features.

Developed as a healthcare management SaaS project at Aligarh Institute of Technology.

Engineering foundation

Built as a real software system, not just a marketing shell.

FrontendHTML5, CSS3, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript, PWA manifest and service worker.
BackendPHP 8 with server side business logic and role based routing.
Data layerMySQL with core relational entities for clinic operations.
AI servicesPHP-based AI logic with a Python service module for extended workflow features.
CommunicationWhatsApp deep-link reminders and an SMS notification toggle in admin.
Security layerRBAC, audit logs, HTTPS and controlled session handling.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-01-01

What we collect

Clinic account data (name, email, phone), patient data entered by the clinic (name, contact, visit history), and server logs. We do not run third-party tracking analytics.

Where it is stored

On the hosting environment chosen by the clinic, with encrypted backups retained for disaster recovery.

Who can access it

Only authenticated clinic staff within the same clinic. Access is role-scoped and logged.

Retention

Patient and operational records remain until the clinic deletes them or cancels the account. Logs rotate on a 90-day cycle.
